# Third-party assets

The following audio assets retain their own licenses and are not relicensed under the project's GPL license.
Minecraft requires Vorbis OGG resources. The bundled Hallucination-specific file is a renamed copy of Freesound's public OGG preview. The two other files were converted from their original WAV downloads to Vorbis OGG and renamed for use by the mod.

- Hallucination-specific ambience: ["Percussive_16BarWeirdness.wav"](https://freesound.org/people/kapnos_mantis/sounds/212921/) by [kapnos_mantis](https://freesound.org/people/kapnos_mantis/), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). No changes were made beyond Freesound's preview transcoding and renaming the file for use by the mod.
- Shared Hallucination and Dread ambience: ["FX one shot 9 -150 bpm - G min - STTTL"](https://freesound.org/people/STTTL/sounds/829229/) by [STTTL](https://freesound.org/people/STTTL/), dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Dread-specific ambience: ["Acid audio effect"](https://freesound.org/people/Vicces1212/sounds/839940/) by [Vicces1212](https://freesound.org/people/Vicces1212/), dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
