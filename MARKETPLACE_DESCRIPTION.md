<h1 align="center">Substance</h1>

Substance is a humorous Minecraft mod about fictional substances, questionable chemistry, smokables, and items inspired by *Breaking Bad*. Grow or find ingredients, process them through multistep production chains, and use the finished products for distinct gameplay, visual, and audio effects.

> **Disclaimer:** Substance is a fictional work made for entertainment. It does not encourage or promote real-life drug use, unsafe behavior, or illegal activity.

## What the mod adds

- Growable herb, tobacco, ephedra, and chili plants
- Herbal rolls, cigarettes, crystals, and reusable pipes with five ingredient slots
- White and blue crystal production chains using flasks, gas bottles, chemical fluids, and drying trays
- Seven custom effects: Haze, Warp, Keen, Relaxation, Surge, Hallucination, and Dread
- Custom post-processing shaders, audio effects, ambient sounds, and harmless apparitions
- Normal, extended, strong, splash, and lingering potions for every effect
- New villager and wandering trader offers, chest loot, piglin bartering, and mob equipment
- An advancement tree that guides you through farming, smoking items, pipes, chemistry, and effects
- Server-synchronized gameplay settings and separate client controls for visual and audio effects

Substance works as a standalone content mod. Most recipes can be viewed with EMI, JEI, or REI.

## Optional Create integration

[Create Fabric](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-fabric) adds mechanical processing and automation for many production steps. Mix chemical fluids, crush and wash ingredients, fill trays, and press finished crystals as part of a Create production line.

Create is optional. Substance includes manual recipes for its progression when Create is not installed.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Fabric Loader 0.19.2 or newer
- Java 17 or newer
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- [Yet Another Config Lib (YACL)](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl) 3.6.6 or newer

[Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) is optional and provides in-game access to Substance's configuration screen. Without it, settings can be changed in `.minecraft/config/substance.json` while the game is closed.

Only Fabric 1.20.1 is supported at present. Support for newer Minecraft versions and NeoForge is planned, but not guaranteed.
